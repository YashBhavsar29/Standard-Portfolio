I've spent the last three years learning what it actually takes to ship a game — not just a prototype that works in the editor, but a system that survives real players, app store requirements, and deadlines. Carrom Legends taught me what publishing really demands; game jams and web experiments keep me quick at solving problems I haven't solved before.

I mostly focus on building modular, event-driven architecture to keep the code standardised and clean, following established guidelines and principles rather than ad-hoc shortcuts — it's the layer that never shows up in a trailer, but it's what makes a project maintainable past day one.
