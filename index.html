About

I'm Yash Bhavsar, a game developer with 3+ years of hands-on experience building games and interactive experiences - across personal projects, internships, and collaborative development. My primary focus is Unity, where I work across both 2D and 3D, turning gameplay ideas into systems people can actually play.

I'm currently pursuing a B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, while continuing to build, test, and ship through practical projects alongside it.

My Craft - Game Development

Unity is my primary engine, and C# is the language I reach for most. Over time I've worked across gameplay mechanics, player interactions, game rules and turn management, UI integration, and modular system architecture - along with AI-controlled opponents, animation, physics, and the systems that tie it all together into a working game.

My experience spans both independent development and working within teams during internships and collaborative projects. Independent work pushes me to own the entire pipeline, from design through polish. Team projects teach a different skill - fitting into an existing codebase, adapting to shared systems, and building alongside other developers.

I'm also exploring Three.js and browser-based 3D, carrying the same systems-thinking into interactive experiences built for the web.

Unity C# Gameplay Systems AI & NPC Behaviour Physics & Animation Three.js

The Side Quest - Cosmic Dream Studio

Beyond individual projects, I'm working toward something bigger: an independent game studio of my own. Through Cosmic Dream Studio, I'm learning what it actually takes to carry a game from an idea to something published - not just the code, but the product thinking, creative direction, and the discipline of shipping something other people can play.

The long-term goal is to grow the studio past solo development, and eventually bring together a team of people who care about making games as much as I do. I'm not there yet, but it's the throughline behind most of what I build.
